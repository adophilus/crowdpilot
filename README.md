# CrowdPilot (working title)

> A co-pilot for small and medium businesses. It builds your business's taste profile from Qloo and through chat it decides (what to stock, play, serve, host), creates (posts tailored to that profile), and ships (publishes to X/IG/FB). It also keeps you ahead of your business's landscape: trending artists, new spots nearby, events worth attending or sponsoring.

**Competition:** [Qloo Agentic Hackathon](https://qloo.devpost.com) — deadline **Oct 30, 2026, 11:45pm EDT**.

## The one-line pitch

Everyone else in this hackathon builds advisors. CrowdPilot ships: it advises from Qloo's taste graph, then *publishes the post* to a real social account.
